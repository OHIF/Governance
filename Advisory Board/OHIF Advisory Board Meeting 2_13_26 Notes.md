# **OHIF Advisory Board – Meeting Notes**

**Date:** February 13, 2026

---

## **1\. Community Quality & Feedback**

**Discussion**

* Need better visibility into community concerns around quality and upgrade friction.  
* Current signals (GitHub, Slack) are reactive and anecdotal.

**Suggestions**

* Draft a community survey and explore ways to expand response collection of surveys  
* Evaluate feasibility of optional telemetry. Key metrics:  
  * Version number  
  * Device/browser  
  * Extension usage

---

## **2\. Extension Management**

**Discussion**

* Extension workflows are powerful but complex.  
* Opportunity to improve tooling and documentation.  
* Review best practices from 3D Slicer.

**Suggestions**

* Meet with 3D Slicer team to review extension model.  
* Identify UX and tooling improvements for OHIF extension management.

---

## **3\. Contributor Workflow & PR Efficiency**

**Discussion**

* Large PRs are time-consuming to review when design alignment happens late.  
* Need earlier collaboration during design/requirements phase.  
* Possible funding model: industry sponsors maintainer review time.

**Suggestions**

* Define lightweight pre-PR design review process.  
* Draft/revisit guidance for substantial contributions.  
* Explore sponsored maintainer review model.

---

## **4\. Breaking Changes & Release Communication**

**Discussion**

* Breaking changes create friction for adopters.  
* Need clearer process and earlier communication.

**Suggestions**

* Document formal breaking change procedure.  
* Announce major changes further in advance.  
* Provide upgrade guidance with breaking releases.

---

## **5\. Advisory Collaboration**

**Discussion**

* Need centralized space for advisor documentation and discussion.  
* Improve continuity and transparency between meetings.

**Suggestions**

* Create shared advisor docs/discussion area.
